# Product Backlog

1. As a user, I want to see all the countries organized by population (largest to smallest)
2. As a user, I want to see all the cities organized by population 
3. As a user, I want to see the top N cities in the world

## Medium priority (sprint 2)
4. As a user, I want to see all the capitals organized by population 
5. As a user, I want to see the population of a continent 

## Low priority (sprint 3)
6. As a user, I want to see the percentage of people who speak Chinese
7. As a user, I want to see the population of a district.
